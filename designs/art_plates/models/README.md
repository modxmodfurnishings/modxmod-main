# Art Plates models

The purpose of supplying these models is to assist in providing reference assemblies. They are exported in two different formats:

- `.step` meshes exported directly from CAD, and
- `.stl` models processed through a slicer

See also the [Mod Block models](../../mod_block/models/README.md).

## Rationale

By providing models of the 102x96mm Art Plates, artists can install their own designs into Mod Blocks. Product designers may also adapt Art Plates to provide interesting apertures or devices.

As discussed in the [Art Plates README](../README.md), 102mm is effectively a 4" width which is common in square coasters. The 96mm height is preferred because with the box cap removed the top of the art will sit flush with the top edge of the Mod Block.

You may simply design using 4" square coasters, realizing a small top portion of the design will be tucked into the cap.

## File descriptions

The main source files are:

| Filename          | Type | Description                                   |
|-------------------|------|-----------------------------------------------|
| AP102-14inch.step | STEP | Assembled Art Plates as one 1/4" part.        |

These have been converted into flattened models:

| Filename           | Type | Description                                  |
|--------------------|------|----------------------------------------------|
| AP102-14inch.stl   | STL  | Assembled 1/4" Art Plate.                    |
| AP102-2x18inch.stl | STL  | Separated 1/8" Art Plates in 2 parts.        |

## Recommended use

### 3D printed Art Plates

3D printing is one of the easiest ways to produce the Art Plates.

Printing a full 1/4" plate will allow you to create some interesting fill patterns. See the [Art Plate examples](../README.md).

### CNC milled Art Plates

It is possible to mill out a relief design. The edges of your Art Plate should be no thicker than 6.5mm, meaning the central part of your design could project forward beyond the front of the Mod Block when installed.

Also, you may desire to mill a 6mm solid wood canvas instead so you can paint on it. The built-up paint will likely add up to 0.5mm thickness. *Note: consider reducing the size of the Art Plate to 101mm on the long dimension in case paint runs over the edges.*

### Laser cut Art Plates

Laser cutting is one of the most efficient ways to make Art Plates. In fact, the production Mod Blocks ship with two 1/8" laser-cut plywood designs.
