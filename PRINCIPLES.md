# Open Furninshings System Principles

The guiding principles of the Open Furshishings System (OFS) help to ensure decisions we all make are in alignment.

## How to use the Principles

Principles are listed in descending order of importantance: if there are two competing principles, the first principle should be valued more. However, exceptions may always be made to provide the best experience.

## Principle 1: People First

Solving for people's needs is primary goal. Any piece of furniture or the shape of an accessory can be modified to meet an individual's needs, even if it means less interoperability with other OFS Components.

## Principle 2: Compete on quality and artistry

OFS is primarily about common product sizes and attachment points where two products may connect. Creators and businesses are still able to create products with proprietary technology, copyright, and design patents. However, because the general specifications and attachment points are public domain, market competition is driven more on the quality of the products and the inspirational artistry in the design. Businesses should be encouraging interoperability even while making profits.

## Principle 3: Interoperability

Favor permitting connection between two products by using OFS attachment points and connectors, or using common dimensions. This empowers people to reconnect products and accessories to their needs.

## Principle 4: Simple products, complex solutions

Products should be relatively simple by themselves, but come together to solve complicated needs. For example, two medium cabinets, two smaller cabinets, and a tabletop provide a custom height desk for wheelchair-bound user.
