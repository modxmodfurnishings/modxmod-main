# Naming of Things

The Open Furniture System (OFS) needs a standard set of language to ensure interoperability.



## Nouns

### Accessory

A product which is designed to support the functionality of another product, or a product with limited functionality. Hooks and shelves can be accessories, trays and stands can be accessories. Tools may also be accessories.

### Assembly

Two or more parts joined together in some fashion so they function as a whole. A sufficiently complex assembly may be considered a component. An assembly can be made up from sub-assemblies.

### Attachment Point

The physical location that can accept a connector. Holes and slots are attachment points.

### Bundle

A set of products and accessories sold as a single purchasable unit.

### Cabinet

A sturdy box designed for holding significant weight of items inside it. It is open on at least one side. A cabinet may have doors to temporarily seal the cabinet.

### Case

A box containing a product. A case can be simple (chipboard box, cardboard box) or more durable (wood case, metal case).

### Component

An assembly which performs a useful role in a product.

### Connector

A part of a specific shape that permits the joining of two products. The connector can be a product itself. The studs on a hanging panel that mate with an attachment point are connectors. A screw by itself is generally not a connector because it is a part in a kit.

### Fastener

Simple parts that hold two materials together or join products. Screws and locking cams are popular fasteners. Fasteners are generally not considered connectors because they are not sold individually. Washers on fasteners are not considered to be part of the fastener.

### Kit

A set of parts and components which are not fully assembled to create the full product, or a set of parts that support the construction of the product. A product may come partially disassebled as a kit to enable easier shipping, or a set of fasteners may be included with a product as a kit.

### Package

A shippable box containing a product, a kit, a bundle. To protect the interior contents, all items may be in a case.

### Part

Any single item or set of items used to make larger assemblies and components. A screw is a part, a bracket is a part, a software module is a part. Parts have defined boundaries; data and designs do not generally have boundaries. Parts are more than raw materials.

### Product

The primary thing that a user purchases that has useful functionality that a user buys. A drawer front is a product, a cabinet is a product, a soft-close door is a product, a software app is a product.

### Raw Material

The basic materials such as wood, metal, paper, and plastic filament, that are not yet in the shape of a functional part.

### Shelf

A platform with a floor and optional walls that holds significant weight of items placed on it. A shelf is generally part of a product or it is an accessory if sold separately.



## Verbs and Actions

### Connect, Disconnect

"To connect" is to join two products or components together by means of a connector.

### Fasten

"To fasten" is to use fasteners to join two parts, two assemblies, two components, or two products together.
